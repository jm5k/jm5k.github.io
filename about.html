About the Linear Clock Lab

What is the Linear Clock Lab?

The Linear Clock Lab is a suite of minimalist, static, dependency-free time and productivity tools by jm5k. Everything runs client-side and stays local-first in your browser—no backend, analytics, tracking, or frameworks—focusing on clarity, speed, and control.

The current suite includes the Main 24-Hour Linear Clock, Minimal Clock, Task Planner Linear Clock, Multi-Clock, Clock Colors / Preset Themes, FocusLine, Stopwatch, Timer, Time Calculator, Date Calculator, Time Zone Converter, Dashboard, and To Do Lists.
